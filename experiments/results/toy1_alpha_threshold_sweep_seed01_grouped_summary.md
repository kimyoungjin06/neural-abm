This sweep is intended to inspect phase behavior across social influence strength and peer threshold. Treat single-seed sweeps as pilot results.

  mixers. Higher `alpha` increases consensus and slightly improves accuracy.
- The tested thresholds `0.6`, `0.8`, and `0.95` barely separate same-init
  output, latent, or parameter paths because the peer graph is already connected
  under those similarity rules.

  `alpha = 0.5`.

  whole grid: fragmentation remains `50`, so no social parameter update is
  effectively applied.

  include much lower thresholds such as `-0.2`, `0.0`, `0.2`, and `0.4`, or add
  an explicit parameter-alignment path.
