# Pivot-feedback lean H3 successor design v1

Date: 2026-08-06

Status: `DRAFT_SOURCE_FULL_CORE_TRANSFER_RUNNING_NOT_COMPLETE_NOT_AUTHORITY`

## Decision

The current full cascade route is closed at
`BLOCKED_E_G1_INPUT_SUPPORT_OR_ESTIMATOR_FAILURE_NO_ROUTE_PROMOTION`. A future
successor should not reproduce the full H1-H6 program as one all-or-nothing
study. Its first confirmatory question should be limited to H3:

> After an independently defined attention surge, does topic entry peak after
> later-cohort persistence has begun to decline?

The primary object remains a field-balanced episode timing gap. Attention is
needed to define the episode clock, but H1 is not a second confirmatory claim.
H2 and H4 peer/capacity contrasts, H5 transport ratio, H6 neural added value,
simulation, and policy interpretation are outside this successor's first
confirmatory stage.

This document is a scientific design brief. It is not an executable
preregistration, a new gate, an active-state successor, or permission to read
any current or future outcome row.

## Prospective result-space update

While source transfer remains operationally separate, the outcome-free
prospective result space was implemented and independently replayed. The compact
receipt is
[`pivot_feedback_lean_h3_result_space_v1_20260806.summary.md`](../../experiments/evidence/results/pivot_feedback_lean_h3_result_space_v1_20260806.summary.md).
It used no empirical, current G1, model-selection, holdout, or private G4 row.

The exercise did not merely confirm the preferred positive pattern. It included
positive, null, negative, unidentified, compositional, measurement, and
recruitment-mixture cases plus a deterministic adversarial parameter search.
The raw and kernel earliest-peak estimators agreed on the easiest cases, but the
kernel estimator failed sharply on broad and bimodal curves. In the canonical
bimodal recruitment case it passed all 128 repetitions despite an observable
truth gap of zero. The raw estimator avoided that false-positive decision but
still showed weak coverage and near-universal aggregation-paradox flags in the
same case.

Accordingly, the kernel estimator is not admissible as the v1 primary. The raw
estimator is retained only as an unselected candidate, and no support rule is
selected. The next outcome-free design milestone is a generator-held-out blind
synthetic challenge. A hierarchical candidate may be preregistered for that
comparison, but cannot be introduced as a post-hoc rescue.

Most importantly, selective composition and measurement shift each reproduced
the positive observable timing pattern without delayed feedback. Even a future
precise empirical H3 estimate would therefore remain a bounded timing result;
it would not identify a feedback mechanism. The synthetic exercise does not
authorize source-row decoding or confirmatory execution and establishes no
scientific effect.

### Blind challenge precommit

The next synthetic step is frozen in
[`pivot_feedback_lean_h3_blind_challenge_v1.yaml`](../../experiments/configs/pivot_feedback_lean_h3_blind_challenge_v1.yaml).
It is a single-operator procedural blind, not an independent-team blind. Code
and one admissibility rubric must be committed before a fresh 256-bit challenge
seed or any panel is created. The estimator may read only opaque panel rows and
the public generation receipt; generator family, truth, feedback status, and
seed remain private until the blind estimation receipt exists.

The challenge retains the raw estimator as an unselected incumbent and the
kernel estimator only as a negative comparator. Hierarchical partial pooling is
not sufficiently defined and is therefore forbidden in this round rather than
introduced after seeing results. All seven criteria must pass without
post-unblinding threshold changes: valid rate, meaningful-positive detection,
nonpositive false positives, overall and worst-family interval coverage, sign
reversal, and nonidentifiable-case passing. A passing estimator becomes only
eligible for a later freeze; it is not automatically selected, and no support
rule is selected by this exercise.

### Blind challenge result

The precommit was fixed at `5b5f7ef` before the assessment seed or any panel
existed. The completed, independently replayed result is
[`pivot_feedback_lean_h3_blind_challenge_v1_20260806.summary.md`](../../experiments/evidence/results/pivot_feedback_lean_h3_blind_challenge_v1_20260806.summary.md).
Neither frozen estimator was admissible. Raw retained strong directional
behavior but failed overall interval coverage, worst-family interval coverage,
and nonidentifiable-case passing. Kernel failed those criteria and sign
reversal. No estimator or support rule was selected.

This is a substantive estimator failure rather than a near-threshold governance
artifact. Raw coverage was `0.8133` overall and `0.5352` in the asymmetric
peak-and-tail family, against frozen floors of `0.90` and `0.80`. It also passed
`0.0833` of registered nonidentifiable panels against a `0.05` ceiling. The
opened challenge is now discovery-only and may not be reused as blind
confirmation.

The next methodological object is not a lower support floor or a post-hoc
hierarchical rescue. It is an uncertainty-aware peak-set estimator that can
propagate within-episode peak-location uncertainty and explicitly return
`INVALID` for ties, flatness, boundaries, or informative missingness. Any such
candidate must be committed before a fresh, disjoint synthetic blind challenge.
Empirical row access and confirmatory execution remain unauthorized.

### Peak-set development precommit

The development-only candidate is frozen in
[`pivot_feedback_lean_h3_peak_set_development_v1.yaml`](../../experiments/configs/pivot_feedback_lean_h3_peak_set_development_v1.yaml).
It uses Jeffreys-binomial rate draws to form episode-level posterior entry and
persistence peak distributions, then estimates the field-balanced mean of their
draw-wise peak-location gaps. The interval propagates both posterior
peak-location uncertainty and field-stratified episode resampling. This
synthetic likelihood is not yet an empirical likelihood: overdispersion,
repeated-author dependence, and cluster structure remain unresolved.

The candidate fails closed when a registered denominator is zero, a modal peak
is on the event-time boundary, its modal probability is below `0.60`, or its
90% peak set is noncontiguous or spans more than one grid step. Each field must
retain at least four of eight valid episodes. These rules and the diagnostic
draw count are fixed before reading the opened labels through the new
candidate.

The prior challenge can now be used only as an opened discovery diagnostic.
Candidate estimates must be written before its private labels are read, and the
subsequent comparison is descriptive: it has no pass/fail threshold, cannot
establish admissibility, and cannot select an estimator or support rule. A
fresh, disjoint generator and a separate precommit remain necessary for any
future blind promotion. Empirical rows, confirmatory execution, and scientific
effect claims remain forbidden.

### Peak-set opened diagnostic result

The candidate was committed at `ea9bac8` before its estimates were produced.
The completed descriptive result and independent metric replay are recorded in
[`pivot_feedback_lean_h3_peak_set_development_v1_20260806.summary.md`](../../experiments/evidence/results/pivot_feedback_lean_h3_peak_set_development_v1_20260806.summary.md).
The estimator wrote 4,096 estimates before reading any private label file.

Among identifiable panels, 1,084 of 2,560 (`0.4234`) were valid. Conditional on
that restricted set, 95% interval coverage was `0.9723`, meaningful-positive
detection was `0.9376`, the nonpositive false-positive rate was `0`, and sign
reversal was `0.0078`. This is promising conditional calibration, not a direct
win over the raw estimator: raw estimated every panel, while the peak-set
candidate excluded 57.66% of identifiable panels.

Missing-time and monotone-boundary cases all failed closed. Plateau/near-tie
cases were only partly resolved: 440 of 512 were invalid, while 72 remained
valid but made no positive decision. Staggered field mixtures were almost
entirely invalid (2 of 512 valid), showing that a scalar field-balanced mean
does not resolve heterogeneous field timing. Meanwhile, 375 of 427 valid
no-feedback mimics passed, reinforcing that the observable timing estimand
cannot identify feedback.

No new pass/fail gate was applied, and the candidate was neither adjudicated
nor selected. Its low, family-dependent validity is an estimator result rather
than a governance failure. Before a fresh blind challenge, the next prospective
design must address practical near-tie equivalence and field-vector or partial
identification without retuning this candidate on the opened challenge.

### Set-identified prospective design v2

The next definition family is frozen for analytic fixtures only in
[`pivot_feedback_lean_h3_set_identified_prospectus_v2.yaml`](../../experiments/configs/pivot_feedback_lean_h3_set_identified_prospectus_v2.yaml).
It does not replay any opened challenge panel. Its one-percentage-point
probability margin is anchored to the already outcome-blind G0 v4 practical
null, not chosen from peak-set diagnostic metrics. That reuse is provisional:
the margin is dimensionally compatible with binary probabilities but is not a
literature-mandated or empirically validated peak-location threshold.

For an analytic curve, every registered time within the inclusive margin of
the maximum belongs to its practical peak set. An adjacent near tie therefore
becomes a set-valued peak instead of an earliest-argmax tie-break or an automatic
discard. Entry and persistence sets induce an episode gap set; its lower and
upper bounds are averaged within each field. The four field intervals are the
primary estimand vector, while their field-balanced interval hull is only a
secondary summary. No binary pass/fail decision is permitted.

Missing registered times, practically flat curves, and peak sets touching the
event-window boundary remain invalid. A noncontiguous interior peak set is
preserved and labeled multimodal rather than collapsed to one time. Field
heterogeneity is likewise retained: a vector such as `[-1, 0, 1, 2]` may have a
scalar average of `0.5`, but it remains mixed and receives no global positive
decision.

This is definition conformance, not a stochastic estimator. Posterior peak-set
construction, simultaneous uncertainty, and empirical dependence or
overdispersion remain pending a separate precommit. Until those are specified,
the opened challenge, fresh blind generation, empirical rows, and confirmatory
execution remain forbidden.

## What the completed route established

The completed route established a reproducible measurement and partition
pipeline and a real confirmatory feasibility failure. It did not estimate H3
or establish a null, association, prediction, mechanism, transport result,
causal effect, or scientific effect.

The current G1 result may identify general design risks such as rare outcomes
and uneven episode support. Its episode-, field-, topic-, and year-specific
counts must not be used to select the successor scope, thresholds, or reserve
order.

## Claim ceiling

The strongest first-stage claim admissible after a successful successor is:

> In the preregistered populations and episodes, the timing of observed topic
> entry lagged the onset of decline in the registered later-cohort persistence
> measure.

This is a bounded observational timing claim. It does not establish hype,
social learning, information receipt, belief updating, a peer-feedback
mechanism, causal influence, general cross-science transport, or NABM
necessity.

## Minimal lifecycle

Only five milestone transitions are needed.

1. **New-source intake.** Bind a new immutable snapshot or external dataset
   whose complete follow-up covers every candidate primary and sensitivity
   horizon.
2. **Disjoint discovery and precision design.** Use a discovery-only cohort,
   disjoint from the future confirmatory cohort and from the opened current
   G1 cohort, to evaluate measurement behavior and plan precision.
3. **One confirmatory freeze.** Freeze the exact source receipt, population,
   episode construction, estimand, estimator, support rule, uncertainty rule,
   missing-unit action, sensitivity family, and claim ceiling in one contract.
4. **H3 confirmatory execution.** Run support checks, materialization, and the
   registered H3 estimator without adding H1, H2, H4, or model-score branches.
5. **Conditional expansion.** Only a passed, interpretable H3 result may
   motivate separately preregistered mechanism, model, or cold-holdout work.

No microstep needs a new gate-state version. Immutable receipts, an execution
log, one independent result replay, and a human-facing decision summary are
sufficient within each milestone.

## Estimand-aligned support design

The previous fixed 500/100/100 rule must not be copied automatically. The new
support rule must be derived from the exact H3 estimator and frozen before
confirmatory outcomes are read.

At minimum, precision planning must address:

- the episode-by-response-cohort counts that locate the entry peak;
- persistent and non-persistent entry counts needed to locate decline onset;
- uncertainty in each episode timing gap rather than total risk-row volume;
- the minimum number of independently supported episodes per field;
- the target width or directional error probability of the field-balanced
  interval;
- sensitivity to tied peaks, flat trajectories, and boundary-year onsets;
- the exact treatment of a structurally unsupported episode; and
- whether a hierarchical or partial-pooling estimator is permitted.

If partial pooling is used, its model, priors or penalties, diagnostics, and
failure action must be frozen. If unsupported episodes are excluded, the
maximum permitted number and the resulting claim downgrade must be frozen.
Neither rule may be introduced after confirmatory results are inspected.

A scalar event floor may remain as an engineering safeguard, but it cannot
substitute for estimator-specific precision analysis or serve as a universal
scientific threshold.

## Source and cohort requirements

The future preregistration must demonstrate all of the following before
confirmatory row access:

- immutable source identity and complete horizon maturity;
- no reuse of the current 2013-2016 G1 rows or episodes;
- no use of the current 2020-2022 G4 membership or outcomes;
- outcome-blind structural field and episode selection;
- strict separation of discovery and confirmatory cohorts;
- author-overlap handling fixed before labels;
- no reserve activation based on current or successor confirmatory outcomes;
- no fabricated model-selection cohort; and
- a byte-sealed optional cold holdout only if later model work is actually
  authorized.

The presence of a newer snapshot is necessary but not sufficient. If its
eligible H3 outcomes remain too rare for the precision target, the successor
must stop before confirmatory execution rather than broaden definitions or
lower support requirements opportunistically.

## Required future preregistration contents

One machine-readable contract should contain only the claim-bearing choices:

1. source and maturity receipt;
2. population and disjoint partition identities;
3. theory-led episode rule and clock;
4. primary persistence definition and sensitivities;
5. exact timing-gap estimand and estimator;
6. precision-derived support and missing-unit rules;
7. uncertainty, multiplicity, and failure actions;
8. leakage and author-overlap rules; and
9. claim promotion and downgrade language.

Formatting, navigation, test-runner settings, and intermediate engineering
receipts should not become scientific lineage roots unless they change one of
these choices.
