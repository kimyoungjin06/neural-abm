# Pivot-feedback lean H3 successor design v1

Date: 2026-08-06

Status: `DRAFT_PRE_SOURCE_NO_ROW_ACCESS_NOT_AUTHORITY`

## Decision

The current full cascade route is closed at
`BLOCKED_E_G1_INPUT_SUPPORT_OR_ESTIMATOR_FAILURE_NO_ROUTE_PROMOTION`. A future
successor should not reproduce the full H1-H6 program as one all-or-nothing
study. Its first confirmatory question should be limited to H3:

> After an independently defined attention surge, does topic entry peak after
> later-cohort persistence has begun to decline?

The primary object remains a field-balanced episode timing gap. Attention is
needed to define the episode clock, but H1 is not a second confirmatory claim.
H2 and H4 peer/capacity contrasts, H5 transport ratio, H6 neural added value,
simulation, and policy interpretation are outside this successor's first
confirmatory stage.

This document is a scientific design brief. It is not an executable
preregistration, a new gate, an active-state successor, or permission to read
any current or future outcome row.

## What the completed route established

The completed route established a reproducible measurement and partition
pipeline and a real confirmatory feasibility failure. It did not estimate H3
or establish a null, association, prediction, mechanism, transport result,
causal effect, or scientific effect.

The current G1 result may identify general design risks such as rare outcomes
and uneven episode support. Its episode-, field-, topic-, and year-specific
counts must not be used to select the successor scope, thresholds, or reserve
order.

## Claim ceiling

The strongest first-stage claim admissible after a successful successor is:

> In the preregistered populations and episodes, the timing of observed topic
> entry lagged the onset of decline in the registered later-cohort persistence
> measure.

This is a bounded observational timing claim. It does not establish hype,
social learning, information receipt, belief updating, a peer-feedback
mechanism, causal influence, general cross-science transport, or NABM
necessity.

## Minimal lifecycle

Only five milestone transitions are needed.

1. **New-source intake.** Bind a new immutable snapshot or external dataset
   whose complete follow-up covers every candidate primary and sensitivity
   horizon.
2. **Disjoint discovery and precision design.** Use a discovery-only cohort,
   disjoint from the future confirmatory cohort and from the opened current
   G1 cohort, to evaluate measurement behavior and plan precision.
3. **One confirmatory freeze.** Freeze the exact source receipt, population,
   episode construction, estimand, estimator, support rule, uncertainty rule,
   missing-unit action, sensitivity family, and claim ceiling in one contract.
4. **H3 confirmatory execution.** Run support checks, materialization, and the
   registered H3 estimator without adding H1, H2, H4, or model-score branches.
5. **Conditional expansion.** Only a passed, interpretable H3 result may
   motivate separately preregistered mechanism, model, or cold-holdout work.

No microstep needs a new gate-state version. Immutable receipts, an execution
log, one independent result replay, and a human-facing decision summary are
sufficient within each milestone.

## Estimand-aligned support design

The previous fixed 500/100/100 rule must not be copied automatically. The new
support rule must be derived from the exact H3 estimator and frozen before
confirmatory outcomes are read.

At minimum, precision planning must address:

- the episode-by-response-cohort counts that locate the entry peak;
- persistent and non-persistent entry counts needed to locate decline onset;
- uncertainty in each episode timing gap rather than total risk-row volume;
- the minimum number of independently supported episodes per field;
- the target width or directional error probability of the field-balanced
  interval;
- sensitivity to tied peaks, flat trajectories, and boundary-year onsets;
- the exact treatment of a structurally unsupported episode; and
- whether a hierarchical or partial-pooling estimator is permitted.

If partial pooling is used, its model, priors or penalties, diagnostics, and
failure action must be frozen. If unsupported episodes are excluded, the
maximum permitted number and the resulting claim downgrade must be frozen.
Neither rule may be introduced after confirmatory results are inspected.

A scalar event floor may remain as an engineering safeguard, but it cannot
substitute for estimator-specific precision analysis or serve as a universal
scientific threshold.

## Source and cohort requirements

The future preregistration must demonstrate all of the following before
confirmatory row access:

- immutable source identity and complete horizon maturity;
- no reuse of the current 2013-2016 G1 rows or episodes;
- no use of the current 2020-2022 G4 membership or outcomes;
- outcome-blind structural field and episode selection;
- strict separation of discovery and confirmatory cohorts;
- author-overlap handling fixed before labels;
- no reserve activation based on current or successor confirmatory outcomes;
- no fabricated model-selection cohort; and
- a byte-sealed optional cold holdout only if later model work is actually
  authorized.

The presence of a newer snapshot is necessary but not sufficient. If its
eligible H3 outcomes remain too rare for the precision target, the successor
must stop before confirmatory execution rather than broaden definitions or
lower support requirements opportunistically.

## Required future preregistration contents

One machine-readable contract should contain only the claim-bearing choices:

1. source and maturity receipt;
2. population and disjoint partition identities;
3. theory-led episode rule and clock;
4. primary persistence definition and sensitivities;
5. exact timing-gap estimand and estimator;
6. precision-derived support and missing-unit rules;
7. uncertainty, multiplicity, and failure actions;
8. leakage and author-overlap rules; and
9. claim promotion and downgrade language.

Formatting, navigation, test-runner settings, and intermediate engineering
receipts should not become scientific lineage roots unless they change one of
these choices.

## Current boundary

Until a new source receipt exists, the only permitted work is source-level
metadata inventory and refinement of this outcome-free design. Current G1
labels, current model-selection rows, private or holdout G4 artifacts, model
fitting, threshold changes, scope rescue, and scientific claim promotion
remain unauthorized. Current state v4 and `g4_open=false` are unchanged.
